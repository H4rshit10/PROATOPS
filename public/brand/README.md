# PROATOPS logo

Drop the logo file(s) here, then tell me the filename.

## Preferred
`logo.svg` — one file, any size, recolourable and animatable.

## Also fine
`logo.png` — transparent background, at least 1200px on the long edge.
I can knock out a solid background and recolour it, but animation is then
limited to moving/scaling the whole mark rather than its individual parts.

## If the logo has a wordmark and a symbol
Supply both separately when possible:
- `logo-mark.svg`  — the symbol alone, roughly square (used for the browser
  icon, the mobile header and the social share image)
- `logo-full.svg`  — symbol + "PROATOPS" lockup (used in the header and footer)

## Reds in this site
The red portion gets matched to whichever surface it sits on:
- `#FF1F2D` on the dark hero (the "Operation." script)
- `#E11D2E` on the light body (section markers, "Proatops Executes.")

Send the file as-is; do not recolour it first.
